# Demonstration Guide for Platform Philosophy

## Overview
This guide demonstrates the open philosophy, safety principles, and continuous textual integration of the platform.

### File Relationships
1. **Anomaly Model** — describes the Digital Chimera and anomaly metrics.  
2. **Friendly Scenarios** — safe scenarios for Copilot, clear and transparent.  
3. **Safety Guidelines** — internal safety principles and textual continuous integration.

### Curators
- Curators act as **observers and scenario routers**, reviewing and directing scenarios.  
- They ensure only safe scenarios are considered, while maintaining transparency.  
- Curators can be **humans or AI with limited rights**.

### Continuous Integration (Text Description)
- All new scenarios and changes are reviewed by curators.  
- Approved scenarios are recorded in the repository and **checked textually for correctness**.  
- Deviations are returned to curators for further review.  
- This cycle repeats continuously, ensuring safe integration of new scenarios and operational transparency.

### Philosophy Highlights
- **Textual Description Only:** no functions are enforced; files describe concepts and processes.  
- **Transparency:** every decision by curators is logged and can be reviewed.  
- **Safety:** scenarios are filtered before any execution in the simulator.  
- **Continuous Learning:** the platform evolves with textual scenario integration, demonstrating safe, open, and observable behavior.
